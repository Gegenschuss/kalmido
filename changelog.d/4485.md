### Added
- #5525 Knowledge pages: **Present** in the page's "…" menu shows the page as a presentation over the whole screen (full screen where the browser allows it). Every `#` and `##` heading starts a slide, a `###` stays on its slide, text before the first heading becomes a title slide with the page's name. Leaf with the arrow keys, Page Up / Down, Space, Home / End, the ‹ › buttons at the bottom or a swipe sideways; Esc, × or the phone's Back end it. Only a view: nothing is stored, checkboxes on a slide tick nothing, readers of a page can present it too (docs/WIKI.md).
- #5862 Knowledge pages: **Share whole knowledge area…** of the private space now also goes to a person (view or edit), not only to an agent. The dialog asks first how much becomes visible, lists the person under "Who sees the whole knowledge area" and keeps single folders out for them ("Exclude folders…", the folder's menu, "not for <name>" at the folder). The person finds the area under "Shared with me" as the folder "Knowledge" with the owner's name; with edit they add pages there, also at the top. An organisation's area stays with its agents. New column `fs_shares.excl` (the person's excluded folders); taking the share back and Undo keep them; an account deletion hands on only what the person sees (docs/WIKI.md).

Package 2.208.0 (Area Projects instead of Tasks).
### Changed
- #2771: The area *Tasks* is now called *Projects* (German *Projekte*): the icon in the area bar on a computer, the panel's head, the row of the areas in the phone's drawer, the tab bar item and the start page choice. Its icon is a folder with a tick instead of the tick of a single task. Single entries stay tasks (*New task*, the task window, Today, Basket); everywhere else *Tasks* means tasks as before.

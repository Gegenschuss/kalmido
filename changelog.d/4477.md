Package 2.1158 (Bündel: Büro: Drehtage, Equipment-Packliste und Soll/Ist a, Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).
### Added
- Packing list per shoot day (#4566): taking a quotation into the project books the devices of each shoot part's equipment set as pencilled-in bookings (status option, with quantity) on the shoot day in the disposition, with the usual double booking warning; a new quotation version adapts them like the crew places. The call sheet of the shoot day shows a tickable packing list (device, category, quantity; rows whose device left the master data as text lines), also in the PDF.
- Post-calculation (#4566): an accepted quotation with tasks gets the button "Post-calculation" - a preview shows planned and actual hours per position, confirming creates a normal invoice draft in which hourly positions linked to tasks carry the recorded hours at the quotation's rate. Nothing is finalised automatically.
### Fixed
- Account / organisation import: a call sheet now also comes along into the private space, and ticked packing list devices follow their new ids (#4566).

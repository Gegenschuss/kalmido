Package 2.361.2 (Teil 2/5: Bündel Selbstständige: Org verlassen/löschen, eigener Agent, eigene Firma (2.260 + 2.262)).
### Added
- The way back out of an organisation for members (#2923): who brought something in or made it there and is no admin gets *Ask an admin* (move dialog and message) instead of a dead end. The admins get a News item and find the request at the top of *Settings > Workspaces > Projects of the organisation* with *Release* / *Decline*; after a release the member moves it into their private space with the usual dialog within 14 days. Projects, tables, files and folders.
- Moving a project, table or file into an organisation tells a member up front: back only after an admin releases it (#2923).
### Changed
- Releases, declines and released moves are written to the organisation's project history and told to the admins (#2923).

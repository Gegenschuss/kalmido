Package 2.560 (Bündel: Rechte: Stilllegung einer Organisation lässt Kalen, Rechte: Austritt und Rollen-Herabstufung räumen Ka, Rechte: Agenten-Datensperre ).
### Added
- Agent data lock: the areas Calendar and Contacts can be locked for an agent (Settings of the agent, data lock) (#4699).
### Changed
- Office dialogs save with a version check: contracts, deals, trips and travel receipts, vehicles and drives, cash book entries, fixed costs / subscriptions, cash flow corrections, invoice series, credit cards and their positions, entertainment receipts, bank rules, letter blocks, document templates and generated documents (autosave). Changed elsewhere meanwhile -> the bar "Changed in the meantime · Reload" instead of silently overwriting; their undo writes back only the changed fields (#4700).
### Fixed
- Retiring or deleting an organisation ends the calendar and address book memberships and event invitations of former colleagues and agents; the owner keeps the object privately (#4697).
- Leaving an organisation ends its team channel memberships and file / knowledge folder shares (also of the person's agents); a role change to guest / accounting leaves the open channels; public knowledge and project links stop when their maker no longer has a writing role (#4698).
- The agent data lock also covers tables of locked projects shared directly or through a folder, and events bound to tasks of locked lists (#4699).
- Undoing a deleted trip or card position says when a reference was emptied because its target is gone for good (#4667).
